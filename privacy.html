ClipNation - Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 November 2026

ClipNation is a personal desktop tool that turns highlights from public livestreams into short vertical videos and, when the account owner approves them, sends them to the owner's own TikTok account. This policy explains what data ClipNation handles.

1. Who operates ClipNation

ClipNation is operated by an individual creator for their own TikTok account. Contact: tomas.ferreira.p11@gmail.com.

2. Data we access from TikTok

  • Basic profile information (display name, avatar, open ID) via the user.info.basic scope, only to show which TikTok account is connected.
  • Access and refresh tokens issued by TikTok after you log in, used only to upload videos you approved (video.upload / video.publish scopes).

ClipNation does not read your followers, messages, likes, viewing history or any other TikTok data.

3. Where data is stored

All data stays on the computer where ClipNation runs. Tokens are stored in a local file and are never sent to any server other than TikTok's official API. ClipNation has no backend server, does not sell or share data, and does not use data for advertising.

4. Public livestream content

ClipNation processes publicly available livestream video, audio and chat activity from Twitch and YouTube to detect highlights. Every published video credits the original creator.

5. Retention and deletion

Temporary video files are deleted automatically after a few days. You can disconnect your TikTok account at any time in the ClipNation panel (Disconnect account), which deletes the stored tokens, and you can also revoke access in the TikTok app under Settings - Security - Manage app permissions.

6. Children

ClipNation is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their data.

7. Changes

We may update this policy; the date above shows the latest version.

8. Contact

Questions or deletion requests: tomas.ferreira.p11@gmail.com.
